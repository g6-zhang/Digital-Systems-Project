The tiger (scientific name: Panthera tigris), commonly known as the "big cat", is one of the largest living felines. Tigers possess keen hearing, exceptional night vision, retractable sharp claws, and powerful canine teeth. Aside from their large size and muscular build, their most distinctive feature is their black vertical stripes on a coat that ranges from white to orange-yellow, which provides effective camouflage while hunting.

Tigers are native to Asia and have an extensive range, inhabiting environments from the coniferous and broadleaf forests of the Sikhote-Alin Mountains in Russia, to open grasslands, and even tropical swamps. Tigers are solitary animals with strong territorial instincts, marking and defending their own territory. They require large home ranges to support an adequate prey population, which often overlaps with human-dense regions, leading to serious conflicts between tigers and human interests.

Historically, there were nine recognized tiger subspecies, but three have already gone extinct. The remaining six subspecies are all endangered, with some classified as critically endangered.At the beginning of the 20th century, the global tiger population was estimated at around 100,000. However, by 2020, the World Wildlife Fund (WWF) estimated that only about 3,900 wild tigers remain. Although government protections exist, illegal poaching remains a major threat.Overall, habitat loss and declining genetic diversity pose severe challenges to the survival of the species, making tigers one of the most vulnerable apex predators on the planet.

The tiger is one of the most widely recognized animals in the world, appearing in numerous ancient myths, folklore, modern films, and various media. Tiger imagery is also commonly featured in flags, coats of arms, and mascots for sporting events.The Bengal tiger is the national animal of Bangladesh and India.The Indochinese tiger is the national animal of Thailand.The Malayan tiger is the national animal of Malaysia.
