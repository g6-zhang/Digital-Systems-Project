Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a refund after booking?

Tickets can be refunded up to 24 hours before your scheduled visit.

Are group discounts available?

Yes, we offer discounts for groups of 10 or more. Please contact us in advance.

Can I book tickets for another day?

Yes, you can select any available future date during the booking process.

Do I need to print my ticket?

No need to print. Just show the e-ticket or QR code on your mobile phone.

What are the zoo’s opening hours?

We are open daily from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, including weekends and holidays.

Are pets allowed inside the zoo?

For safety reasons, pets are not allowed inside the zoo premises.
